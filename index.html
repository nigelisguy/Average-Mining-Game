Welcome, Miner!

Mine blocks around your character, collect ores, and bring them back to the surface. Your goal is to go deeper, get stronger, and build a ridiculous mining empire.

Movement & Mining

Use WASD or the arrow keys to move. Walk into solid blocks to mine them. Click nearby villagers on the surface to accept and turn in quests.

Interacting with NPCs

There is a NPC next to you, it is the Starter NPC, which gives exclusive rewards. Click/Approach him, and click on the quests on the left to accept them!

Skill Tree

Level up by mining to earn Skill Points. Open the Stats tab and click available nodes. A node can only be purchased when every requirement in skills.js is at least level 1.

Achievements

Achievements track milestones like depth, mining, shiny finds, quests, and skill progression. They unlock automatically as you play.

Your Music

Open Settings to upload an audio file from your device and play it while you mine. The browser keeps the selected track for this session; your save file never uploads it anywhere.
